## Opus Decision (summary)

**Winner:** Mutable Dice Engine (Dice Forge × Slice & Dice)

**Key reasoning:**
- Only candidate where the statistical PMF IS the manipulable game piece. Player cannot choose which face to engrave without reasoning about probability mass. Cosmetic degradation is structurally impossible.
- Café Confidence fatal risk: player can anchor on sample mean and ignore CI width, still progressing — stats concept not fully load-bearing in hard-failure mode.
- Pre-Commit Pipeline fatal risk: 60-90 second plan phase violates 15-30s rule; Hebrew RTL planning UI on mobile is an interaction swamp.
- Tightest rhythm of the three (12-20s). Strongest engine-building (every engraved face persists cross-session).

**Board game:** Dice Forge (Libellud, 2017)  
**Mobile game:** Slice & Dice (Tann Gamble, 2021)  
**Hebrew CTA:** חרוט פאה חדשה  
**Top integration risk:** Live PMF histogram reactive to Zustand face array at 60fps — Recharts/D3 re-render thrash is the specific failure mode to prototype first in Cycle 2.
