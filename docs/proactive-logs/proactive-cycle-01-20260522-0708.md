## Key decision: #1 = The Variance Café

**Why:** Highest decision rhythm (15s cadence), strongest "run your own place" emotional pull, and structurally prevents decoration drift — the statistical concept (mean + variance inference from sample) is the only input to the reward function. The histogram IS the gameplay surface, not a decoration layer.

**Core loop summary:** Each shift, customers arrive with a hidden taste distribution. Player sees a sample (2–3 dots), tunes μ/σ sliders, commits a brew, watches satisfaction histogram fill. Tips = distributional overlap. Equipment cards expand the statistical action space.

**Next cycle scope:** Implement μ-only prototype behind `FEATURE_VARIANCE_CAFE` flag. 5 customers, 3 equipment cards, one shift ~90 seconds. Prove the 15s cadence and the "σ too tight" aha moment.
