## #1 Pick: Distribution Forge

**Core premise:** Player crafts a personal "data die" face by face. Die's mean and σ gate contracts (mini-missions). Every game verb — forge, roll, challenge, rival — reads off real die statistics. No cosmetic decoration path.

**Key stats coverage:** mean, std dev, CI, hypothesis testing, regression, sampling, distributions (d6→d20 unlock).

**Reuse:** Existing R3F scene, SM-2 engine, quiz bank, Hebrew lesson content.

**Decision interval:** ~20s (forge bursts to ~8s).
