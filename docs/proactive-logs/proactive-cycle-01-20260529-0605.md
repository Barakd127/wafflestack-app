## Opus design decision summary

**Input brief:** 8 candidate gameplay loops (A–H) with scoring criteria.  
**Output:** Scorecard, top-3 ranking, detailed spec for #1.

### Final ranking

| Rank | Candidate | Score / 30 | Decision interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| #1 | C — Push-Your-Luck Sampling ("The Sampling Cauldron") | 26 | 3–8s per pull decision |
| #2 | A — Mutable-Dice Engine ("Forge Your Distribution") | 25 | 30–60s per forge decision |
| #3 | H — Boss-Battle Encounters ("Statistical Bestiary") | 23 | 15–30s per encounter action |

### Opus key insight for #1

> "The decision *is* the concept with zero translation loss: every pull is a sample, every 'stop' is a stopping rule, and the tension the player feels in their gut — 'is my estimate good enough yet, or do I risk one more pull?' — is literally the sampling-precision tradeoff that intro students fail to *feel* from a textbook."
