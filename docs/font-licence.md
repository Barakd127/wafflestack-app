A second order, **#04350**, is the desktop licence — it covers the design work,
not the site. The website licence above is the one the deployed app runs under.

## Two things to watch

**The domain is named in the licence.** It covers `wafflestack-app.vercel.app`.
A move to a custom domain, or serving the fonts from a second host, is a new
registration at Fontef — the same free form, not an automatic carry-over.

**The tier is traffic-bound.** Under 10,000 monthly visitors. Past that, the
licence steps up.

## Where the documents are

Not in this repo, deliberately. Fontef's licence PDFs carry the licensee's
name, home address and telephone number, and anything committed here is visible
to everyone with repo access, permanently, in the history. The originals stay
with Shirli and Barak; this file carries everything that has to be verifiable.
