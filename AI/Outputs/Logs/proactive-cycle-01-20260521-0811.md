## #1 Pick Summary: Waffle Stack Challenge

Press-your-luck mechanic. Each concept = 3-layer question stack (easy/medium/hard). After each correct answer: **Bank It** (collect tentative XP) or **Push the Stack** (risk it for 2.5× multiplier). Wrong answer mid-stack = full stack collapse, 0 XP.

- Fixes: no in-session decision; no stakes; flat session structure; SM-2 invisibility
- SM-2 quality rating becomes depth reached (L1=3, L2=4, L3=5, collapse=0-2)
- Session: 5 stacks, variable length 4–15 min based on push choices
- Feature flag: `VITE_FEATURE_WAFFLE_STACK=true`
- Thematically native: app is literally called WaffleStack
