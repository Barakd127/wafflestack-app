### 1. The Forecast Desk — "Bet your confidence on real outcomes, get paid in certainty"

**Mechanics:** Push-your-luck + prediction market. Each round shows partial data (a few sample points, a histogram-so-far). The player must call an outcome (will the next sample exceed the mean? is this coin biased? is group A > group B?) AND stake a confidence band (50/80/95/99%). Reveal happens point-by-point with animation; payout scales with how tight a band you dared while staying calibrated. A running "calibration meter" tracks whether your stated confidence matches your actual hit rate.

**Stats concept used in decision:** Confidence levels, sampling distributions, the cost/benefit of certainty, p-values as a stake. Choosing 95% vs 80% IS the statistical decision.

**Decision interval:** ~12–20s between calls

**Board game inspiration:** Quacks of Quedlinburg, mechanic: stop-or-draw push-your-luck where stopping early is safe but low-reward.

## #1 Detailed Spec: The Forecast Desk

### Core loop (step by step)

1. A **scenario card** appears (Hebrew, RTL): a partial dataset — e.g. 6 of 20 sampled exam scores plotted as a growing dot-strip, with a question: "האם הציון הבא יעלה על הממוצע?"
2. Player makes a **binary/categorical call** (swipe or tap: yes / no, or A>B / B>A / no difference).
3. Player **stakes a confidence band**: a 4-stop slider 50% / 80% / 95% / 99%. UI shows live: "stake 95% → payout ×3, but a miss costs ×3."
4. Player hits **Reveal**. Remaining samples animate in one by one (~3s, skippable). Outcome resolves.
5. **Payout** in "Certainty" currency, modified by the band. A persistent **Calibration Meter** updates: of all your 95% calls, what % were right?
6. Every ~5 rounds, a **Tutor interjection** (existing AI Tutor drawer) fires only when calibration is off.
7. Mastered topics unlock **Edge cards** (drawn from Arsenal): "reveal one extra sample before calling," "see the SE," "re-bet once."

### Statistical concepts integrated (and HOW)

| Concept | Mechanic moment |
|---|---|
| Confidence levels | The band-stake slider: 50/80/95/99 = literal α levels |
| Sampling distribution / SE | "See the SE" Edge card; running mean stabilizing as n grows |
| p-value intuition | A>B calls: correct "no difference" at tight band rewards null-hypothesis thinking |
| Calibration | Meta-layer: the meter proves a 95% claim must hit 95% of the time |
| Distributions | Scenarios drawn from named distributions; skew punishes naive mean-based calls |

### Decision moments (the 15–30s rhythm)

- **0–5s:** read the partial data, form a directional prior (the call).
- **5–12s:** the real decision — *how confident am I?* Set the band. This is the concept-bearing choice every round.
- **12–15s:** reveal animation + payout.
- **Every ~3 rounds:** *which Edge card to play now vs save?* — second-order decision.

### Failure states and recovery

- A miss never ends the session — it costs Certainty and dings calibration. The Tutor reframes it as data.
- No wall: a player at 0 Certainty restarts at 50% bands (always safe), guaranteed forward motion.

### Session length

Typical 3–4 min (12–18 rounds). Min: 1 round (~20s). Max: soft cap ~25 rounds.

### Progression arc (across days/weeks)

| Phase | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Binary calls, normal data, bands only. Learn calibration. |
| Week 1 | A-vs-B comparisons; skewed data appears. |
| Week 2+ | Edge-card deck grows from mastered topics. Engine-building. |
| City tie-in | Banked Certainty funds buildings; each building produces a passive Edge card per session — the Godot city becomes an engine input, not cosmetic. |

### Why this beats the current "answer quiz → unlock building" loop

The quiz loop has one decision (pick the right answer) and the building is a cosmetic payout — a textbook VISION violation. The Forecast Desk adds a *second, harder, concept-bearing decision every round*: how confident am I? That choice has no "correct answer" to memorize — it is a live risk judgment that only improves with genuine statistical intuition. You cannot fake calibration. The reward (Certainty) is consumed by the engine, so progress is decision, not decoration.

### Open questions / risks

- **Calibration legibility in Hebrew/RTL:** the meter must read instantly without a tutorial wall.
- **Variance frustration:** even a perfectly-calibrated 80% call loses 20% of the time. Payout tuning + Tutor framing must prevent good play from feeling unlucky.
- **Scenario authoring cost:** can forecast scenarios be auto-derived from existing MC questions?
- **Is binary call too shallow early?** Risk it feels like coin-flipping in week 1 before A/B and Edge cards arrive.
- **Syllabus coverage:** strong on inference/probability; weaker on descriptive stats and regression. Candidate #5 (Correlation Cartographer) is a natural sibling mode.
